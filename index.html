Nychthemeron has shut down.

March 2026 – November 2026

Turns out running a social platform alone is a full-time job. I already have a job, a family and a life, so something had to lose. It was this.

Programming has been my hobby since school, so shutting this down is basically stepping away from the hobby itself. Yes, that part stings.

I could have let it rot quietly with broken features and nobody answering. Instead, it gets a proper burial. Thanks to everyone who posted and watched it all vanish, which was kind of the point anyway.

The code is open source. Want to learn from it, fork it, or run your own? Be my guest, just know what you're signing up for.
